Just as I was about to go to bed, minutes after I made a firm resolution that I will wake up at 6AM for the next two days, I had the intense urge to write the opening post to my new blog "Dumb is okay" (this one). Kind of dumb to do this right now and cut into my sleep time, but I guess it's okay. 

I didn't think that *this* would be my first blog post - honestly I thought it would be a review of a book or a movie. But I have realised that I am not yet cut out to make the deep critiques I imagined. I mean - what do I even know about books/movies really? In spite of having spent a large chunk of my adult life glued to the screen - I probably know too little about movies to put forth an original  perspective. This incompetence is at least partially because I was halfway zoned out (or stoned out) through most of these movies. And as for books, well, to quote the Kings of Convenience - "I haven't read a book all year". 

Maybe I'm mistaken about my reasons for starting this blog. In hindsight, the number of times I changed the name of the blog was probably a big clue. I guess I thought that I could produce something worth "listening" to - but what I was not counting on, was that I had lost my voice - or perhaps I had never found it in the first place. 

Recently, I have been questioning a lot of things, including my self-worth, both in constructive and destructive ways. A new tattoo of a mouse reminds me every day that comparison is the death of joy, and recently I've been wondering - what is that mouse looking at? My conclusion is that it must be cheese. 

While I've been trying to chase the cheese, life keeps throwing me curve balls, mocking my determination and resolve, and my carefully cultivated discipline keeps collapsing like a house of cards at the hint of a breath of wind. My mind, is a raging bull, and it fucking hates me. Or at least, every 3 days it does - that's the period of my oscillation (or so runs the current hypothesis). 

Get a move on! (with your life! with everything!)

I guess that's what I'm trying to do with this blog. Breathe some life back into my dreams. Or am I? Because this feels a bit more like breathing life back into myself. Will I be able to write like this tomorrow? Probably not. Everything is at the mercy of my mood, but at least I now know that anxiety is a good muse, and hell - anxiety won't leave my side even if I tried to pay her off. 

Life is weird, I like watching and reading about the weird lives of other people, so maybe I just need to throw some weirdness back into the pond, and maybe this will attract some weird fishies who get off on weirdness like I do. Randomness on the screen is a refreshing breath of fresh air to me, randomness in music even so - although, I'm not sure how I feel about randomness in the textual medium. After all, my academically trained brain is primed to praise simple, succinct and linear, and reject chaotic non-utilitarian rants like this one. 

But that's okay, I doubt I am my target market anyway. Mostly, because I hardly read - and definitely not blog posts!
